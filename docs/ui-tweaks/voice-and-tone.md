# Voice and tone rules

Keep these in mind so the fixes don't creep back to AI-polish. Read this before editing copy.

## 1. One reassurance per flow

If we say "we don't save your CV," say it once. The user does not need to be told three times. Treat reassurance like salt.

## 2. Concrete verbs, no abstract gerunds

Bad:
- "Build the requirements one at a time"
- "Start building your future"
- "Move you closer"

Good:
- "Get your hydraulic ticket at GTI."
- "Book a results interview."
- "Add a BOSIET cert."

If the verb cannot be answered with "how, specifically?", cut it.

## 3. Don't number steps unless they are operations

"01 / 02 / 03" is a visual trope, not communication. If the user has to *do* the steps in order, number them like a recipe. If they're just grouped concepts, drop the numbers.

## 4. Use the brand's place

This is Guyana. Mention Guyana, GTI, BOSIET, Lusignan, Gecom, CSEC. Specificity is the cheapest anti-AI signal that exists.

## 5. Cut the legal-disclaimer cadence

Instead of:
> "This is a career-planning guide, not a job application. Your CSEC/CXC results can help you prepare; they are not professional qualifications or a job-match score."

Try:
> "This is not a job application — it's the route toward one. If you see a number, it's a planning estimate, not a score."

Strip "not this, not that" triads. They're an AI hedge-tic.

## 6. Status pills: use the user's words

| Now | Better |
|---|---|
| `Processing queued` | `We're reading your CV` |
| `Interview unlocked` | `You can book a slot` |
| `Small win in progress` | `Reading your CV…` |
| `Requires attention` | `We couldn't read that PDF` |

Pills should read like the *user* could say them out loud, not like a project status board.

## 7. Kill the kicker

The tiny `text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.18em] text-accent` label is *the* AI-banner tell. Keep it for one section max (probably the hero) or replace with the section heading itself.

## 8. Avoid "your X, made clear" constructions

- "Your progress, made clear"
- "A clearer route"
- "Experience, recognized"

This Yoda-like inversion is a text-book AI tell. Just say the thing.

## 9. Stop prefixing everything with possessives

- "Your starting plan"
- "Your best-fit routes"
- "Your progress, made clear"
- "Your next milestone"
- "Your experience"

Not every heading needs a "your." Some can be declarative. "The routes that fit you" reads more confident than "Your best-fit routes."

## 10. Read it out loud

If it sounds like something a TEDx speaker would say, rewrite. If it sounds like something Cynthia at GTI would say to a trainee, ship it.
