# Materials Handler / Porter requirements

Checkpoint 40 adds a judge-readable porter pathway based on Guyanese job and
local-content language. In this context, a bare `Porter` is treated as a
materials-handling signal. A hotel porter remains an accommodation-services
signal.

## What the demo role requires

The active **Materials Handler / Porter** role has a 70% threshold. PostgreSQL
calculates the weighted score; mandatory requirements still block interview
eligibility even when the percentage is high.

| Requirement | Weight | Mandatory | Why it is shown |
| --- | ---: | :---: | --- |
| Manual Handling and Safe Lifting | 5 | Yes | Core lifting, carrying, staging, and load-control capability. |
| HSE Awareness | 4 | Yes | Industrial work must follow site HSSE and access procedures. |
| Cargo Handling | 4 | No | Supports receiving, moving, securing, and dispatching materials. |
| Warehouse Operations | 3 | No | Covers stock, labeling, storage, counts, and traceability. |
| Rigging and Slinging | 3 | No | Useful when the work touches slings, shackles, or planned lifts. |
| Forklift Operations | 2 | No | A useful add-on; it is not assumed from the word “porter.” |
| First Aid and CPR | 1 | No | Helpful site readiness, not a porter-specific credential. |

The existing **Warehouse and Logistics Assistant** role also receives
materials-handling and cargo-handling requirements, so the same gap language
appears when a candidate targets warehouse work.

## What the gap means

- Missing **Manual Handling and Safe Lifting** means the CV did not provide a
  confirmed safe-lifting capability. It does not mean the person cannot lift or
  is medically unfit.
- Missing **HSE Awareness** means the applicant should verify basic industrial
  safety preparation before site access.
- Missing **Rigging**, **Forklift**, or **First Aid** means those are useful
  add-ons for the specific employer or worksite, not automatic rejection from
  every porter role.
- The applicant must confirm extracted evidence before any requirement counts.

## Evidence behind the catalogue

- [Guyana National Job Bank — Warehouse Clerk/Porter](https://jobs.gov.gy/2026/Warehouse-Clerk-Porter.html?action=print) describes stock counts, carrying, labeling, packing, picking, and deliveries.
- [Guyana National Job Bank skills catalogue](https://jobs.gov.gy/job_search_by_skill.php) includes heavy lifting, physical condition for lifting, and safe-work skill language.
- [Local Content Register — Warehouse Services Contract](https://lcregister.petroleum.gov.gy/supplier-notice/warehouse-services-contract/) describes material handling, inventory control, traceability, HSSE, and warehouse systems.
- [Local Content Register — Mechanical Handling Equipment](https://lcregister.petroleum.gov.gy/supplier-notice/provision-of-mechanical-handling-equipment/) references lifting frames, trolleys, chain blocks, slings, and shackles.
- [Board of Industrial Training](https://srms.bit.gov.gy/) is the official place to ask about current hands-on training intake. The demo does not claim that a specific manual-handling course is currently available.
